# Creator

Pitch Deck Engine was created by [Jack Dalrymple](https://www.jackdalrymple.com/), Founder of [Cap and Cut](https://capandcut.com/).

The package brings together investor storytelling, evidence review, and slide editing. It helps founders choose what a reader needs to know and show the proof close to each claim.
