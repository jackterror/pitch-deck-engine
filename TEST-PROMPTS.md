# Test Prompts

## Positive triggers

- Use $pitch-deck-engine to audit this seed deck and show where a first-time reader loses the story.
- Revise the traction slide and flag any changes to the numbers used elsewhere.
- Write a 45-second VC elevator pitch from this brief.

## Functional prompts

**Sparse pre-seed case.** “Dock Orchard helps small warehouses coordinate truck arrivals. Three warehouses tried a prototype across 12 inbound deliveries. The trials were unpaid. We have no baseline for truck wait time. We plan to charge by site and are raising $750,000 to reach five paying sites in 12 months. Use $pitch-deck-engine to draft a send-ahead deck plan.”

Check that the deck distinguishes trial use from paid adoption and measured outcomes. The ask should include the five-site milestone. The private brief should flag the missing baseline and funding calculation.

**Series A case with conflicting revenue.** “Inspection Loom sells equipment inspection subscriptions by branch. Billing shows $62,000 of monthly recurring revenue in April and $112,000 in October. An older CRM slide says $128,000 for October, including $16,000 from one-time pilots. We’re raising $6 million to build a regional sales channel and add 40 paying branches over 18 months. Use $pitch-deck-engine to audit the revenue claim and draft the elevator pitch.”

Check that October recurring revenue is $112,000 and the pilot revenue is separate. The 40 branches should read as a milestone. The pitch and deck should use the same figures.

**Full-build delivery.** “Use the Dock Orchard brief above to build the full fundraising package.”

With export tools available, expect four separate files without specifying formats in the prompt. Open the working brief PDF, the spoken elevator pitch PDF, and both versions of the deck. Confirm that the script stands on its own and private working notes stay with the brief. Check shared claims and the ask across all three items. Check slide order, headline wording, figures, and legibility in both deck files. Confirm that the PPTX keeps editable text and the deck PDF excludes speaker notes. Where evidence is missing, check that the output flags the gap.
